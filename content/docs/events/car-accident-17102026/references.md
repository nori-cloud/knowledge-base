## Road Rules 2014

- [Rules 11–13 — application, roads and road-related areas](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.11): The rules generally apply to roads and road-related areas. Whether this residents' car park qualifies requires checking its access and use. Copies checked: [rule 11](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/11), [rule 12](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/12), [rule 13](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/13).
- [Rule 129 — keeping to the far left](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.129): Requires a driver on a road other than a multi-lane road to drive as near as practicable to the far left. For this rule, “road” excludes road-related areas. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/129).
- [Rule 132 — keeping left of centre](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.132): Applies to a two-way road without a dividing line or median strip, subject to exceptions. It does not exclude road-related areas generally, although it excludes specified paths, footpaths and nature strips. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/132).
- [Rule 133 — exceptions](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.133): The width-or-condition exception requires both that keeping left of centre is not practicable and that driving to the right can be done safely. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/133).
- [Rule 351(3) — meaning of driving to the left or right](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.351): The whole vehicle must be to the relevant side. The vehicle's exact position has not been measured. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/351).
- [Rule 33 — making a right turn](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.33): Includes the path for a right turn where there is no turn line. Its application depends on the junction's legal layout and the vehicle's actual path. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/33).
- [Rule 73 — giving way at a T-intersection](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.73): Its application depends on the junction's classification, signs and the roads involved. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/73).
- [Rule 297(2) — clear view](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.297): Requires a clear view of the road and traffic ahead, behind and to each side. It does not itself authorise crossing the centre. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/297).
- [Rule 146 — driving within a marked lane or line of traffic](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#sec.146): The unmarked-lane provision refers to two or more lines of traffic travelling in the same direction. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-rules-2014/146).

### Definitions

The [Road Rules Dictionary](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2014-0758#dict) contains definitions relevant to lane classification. Definitions of “marked lane” and “line of traffic” were checked in [Khowly v Amoud [2020] NSWSC 1445, paragraphs 41–42](https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au/decision/1753f05a32e71efbd007e4f6), a case about a 2017 collision. A line of traffic can include a single vehicle. The exact incident-date definitions, including “multi-lane road”, remain to be verified. No separate definition of “single-lane road” has been verified.

## Road Transport Act 2013

- [Section 117 — negligent driving](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-2013-018#sec.117): Refers to circumstances including the road's condition and use, traffic, and hazards. This is an offence provision, not a finding of liability or a definition of “practicable”. [Copy checked](https://www.barristerai.com.au/legislation/jurisdiction/nsw/road-transport-act-2013/117).

## NSW driving guidance

These publications provide safety and test guidance, not a universal legal kerb clearance.

- [Hazard Perception Handbook, January 2023, pages 32–33](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2021-08/hazard-perception-handbook.pdf#page=34): Safe space beside other vehicles, including oncoming vehicles on narrow roads.
- [A Guide to the Driving Test, May 2026, page 14](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2021-08/guide-to-driving-test.pdf#page=16): Buffering, slowing where space is limited, and positioning on crests and curves. See also [limited-vision guidance, page 24](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2021-08/guide-to-driving-test.pdf#page=26).
- [Testing Officer's Manual, version 3, Part C, C6](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2021-09/testing-officers-manual.pdf#page=30): The testing criterion measures distance from oncoming vehicles excluding mirrors.
- [Safe stopping distance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/roads-safety-and-rules/safe-driving/safe-stopping-distance): Road positioning and slowing for blind corners and crests.
- [Intersections](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/roads-safety-and-rules/stopping-giving-way-turning/intersections): Public guidance on T-intersections; see rule 73 for its conditions.

## Related cases

The facts differ from this accident. None establishes fault here; later appeal or treatment history has not been exhaustively checked.

- [Sibley v Kais [1967] HCA 43](https://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/cases/cth/HCA/1967/43.html): Reasonable care at an intersection. The AustLII link returned HTTP 403; the text was checked in a [republication](https://www.barristerai.com.au/case/1967/hca/43).
- [Nievas v Mohammed [2017] NSWDC 168](https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au/decision/595c4e09e4b058596cba8490): Applies the reasonable-care principle to a 2012 intersection collision involving a vehicle and cyclist.
- [Johnson v Savage [2003] NSWCA 244](https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au/decision/549fab113004262463b62f6d): A collision at a curved bridge exit involving speed and crossing to the wrong side.
- [BIG Transport v Hi-Tech Heavy Haulage [2020] NSWDC 924](https://www.caselaw.nsw.gov.au/decision/17c0c9a43af1f02bab84a554): Examines a truck driver's claimed need to drive near the centre against clearance and turning-path evidence.
